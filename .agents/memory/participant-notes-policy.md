---
name: Participant notes design boundary
description: CEPS's reasons for plain-text notes and author-owned editing without expiry.
---

Participant notes should remain plain text, without types, categories, or dropdowns. Do not impose a time window for editing an author's own note.

**Why:** CEPS agreed to keep notes simple at the October 9, 2026 meeting and explicitly ruled out time-limited editing because the app sleeps.

**How to apply:** Preserve this boundary when extending notes. Keep the audit trail for edits instead of introducing an edit-expiry rule.
