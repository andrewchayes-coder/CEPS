In the CEPS app's New Referral Intake flow, make three small cleanup changes.

1. Remove the entire "Diagnosis & Eligibility" section from step 4
   (Participant) of the intake form — CEPS does not collect this information.
   Remove the associated form fields, validation, and any place downstream
   that reads or displays them (e.g. the referral detail view), but leave the
   underlying `diagnosis` / `eligibilityCategory` columns on the referrals
   table in place (unused) rather than dropping them, consistent with how
   other deprecated fields have been handled in this codebase.

2. Update the copy on the "Supporting Documents" step (step 5). It currently
   reads: "Optionally attach a supporting document (e.g. diagnosis report,
   POS letter, or eligibility record)." Change it to something like:
   "Optionally upload any invoices, receipts, or other supporting documents
   if available." — CEPS only collects invoice/activity-related documents at
   intake, not diagnosis or eligibility records.

3. Remove the "Received" toggle for authorization status on the Referral
   detail/view screen (the "Mark Received" control tied to
   `altaAuthReceivedAt`). It's easy to trigger by accident, has no undo, and
   is redundant with the participant's Activities screen, which already shows
   whether the authorization has been received. Remove the control; if
   anything downstream reads `altaAuthReceivedAt` to drive a workflow
   transition, confirm that dependency and flag it rather than silently
   breaking it (it's used, for example, to advance a referral from
   pending_auth toward pending_w9/pending_invoice — that transition logic
   should keep working, just triggered from wherever authorization receipt is
   actually recorded elsewhere, e.g. when an authorization record itself gets
   created for that referral's client).

Write or update tests confirming the removed fields/controls no longer appear
and that the referral status-advancement logic that depended on the Received
toggle still functions correctly through its new trigger point.
